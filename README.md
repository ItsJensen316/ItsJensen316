## 🧬 Research Highlights

**Biomarker Identification for Rituximab Resistance in Lymphoma**

During my RGCB research internship, I explored computational approaches to understanding treatment resistance in lymphoma:

- Analyzed transcriptomic data using **R, Python, and DESeq2**
- Investigated differential pathways using **GSEA and Cytoscape**
- Prioritized **PTPN2** as a potential target for further computational investigation
- Evaluated candidate small molecules through **molecular docking and ADMET prediction**

*Research focus: connecting biological insights with computational methods for therapeutic discovery.*
